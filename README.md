## 🌟 About Me

👋 Hi, I'm **Aprajati Siuli**, a **Senior React Native Developer** from 🇮🇳 Kolkata, West Bengal, India.

I have **5+ years** of experience architecting and shipping **scalable, cross-platform mobile apps** across **iOS, Android and WatchOS** — for an international user base, with apps crossing **100,000+ downloads**. I've built production apps for enterprise clients like **DAMAC Properties** alongside consumer health and lifestyle products used by thousands of people worldwide.

I own apps **end to end**: architecture, performance, REST API integration, CI/CD pipelines, and App Store / Google Play publishing — including store listings, ASO, data-safety declarations and review compliance.

- 🔭 **Currently:** Software Development Engineer @ **RHA Technologies Pvt Ltd**
- 🚀 **Just shipped (solo):** **MultiQuiz** & **DailyFinance** — both live on Google Play
- ⚡ **Impact:** Cut app load time by **30%** through memoization, list virtualization & lazy loading
- 🧩 **Beyond the app:** Native modules & bridging, Android/iOS widgets, WatchOS companion apps
- 🌱 **Growing in:** Node.js backend systems & AI/LLM-powered app features
- 🤝 **Open to:** Collaborations, freelance work & exciting opportunities

**🎯 Mission:** *"Ship fast. Ship polished. Ship things people actually keep on their home screen."*
